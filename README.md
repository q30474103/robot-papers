## 🎯 What Is robot-papers?

robot-papers is a **local-first paper reader** built specifically for people interested in embodied AI, robotics, and machine learning. It helps you read, organize, and keep track of research papers about Vision-Language-Action models (VLA), imitation learning, and world models.

Think of it as your personal library that lives on your computer. You download papers, robot-papers keeps them tidy, and its smart timer feature reminds you to review new research on a regular schedule.

This guide is written for everyday computer users. No programming skills are required.

## ✨ Main Features

- **Local-First Storage:** Your papers stay on your computer. No cloud needed, no account required. Your data remains private.

- **Smart Paper Organization:** Automatically sorts papers by topic. Categories include VLA, imitation learning, world models, and more. Research topics appear as clickable tags.

- **Built-in Reader:** Read PDFs directly inside robot-papers. Highlight text, add notes, and mark important paragraphs without leaving the app.

- **Obsidian Integration:** Export your notes and highlights to Obsidian, a popular note-taking tool. Keep everything in one place.

- **Scheduled Updates:** Set a daily or weekly timer. robot-papers automatically checks for new papers and downloads them for you (requires Codex, a free coding assistant).

- **Easy Search:** Type a word or author name. robot-papers instantly finds the paper you need.

- **Works Offline:** Everything functions without internet after the initial download.

## 🔍 Troubleshooting Common Problems

### Problem: The app does not open after download.

Try right-clicking the downloaded file and choosing **Run as administrator**. If Windows SmartScreen blocks it again, click **More info** → **Run anyway**.

### Problem: I cannot find my downloaded papers.

The repository folder you selected at the beginning contains all your files. Open that folder in your file explorer. Every paper appears as a numbered PDF file along with a small `.json` file (metadata). Do not delete the JSON files — they help robot-papers organize the library.

### Problem: Codex updates do not work.

Confirm that your Codex key was entered correctly. Check your internet connection. In **Settings → Codex Integration**, click **Test Connection** to verify everything works.

### Problem: The app feels slow with many papers.

The reader works faster if you close papers you are not reading. Keep the main library limited by archiving old papers into separate folders. Right-click any paper and choose **Move to Archive**.
